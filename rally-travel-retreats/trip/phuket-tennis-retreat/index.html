The experience

A tennis week with enough space to feel like a holiday.

Five mornings of professional coaching at KĀHLĪ Tennis Academy sit at the centre of the retreat. Around that, you've got recovery time at Sole Mio, beaches, food, culture and a private island-hopping day.

Play

5 mornings of professional coaching, up to 2 hours on court per day, physical conditioning and former ATP-ranked coaches.

Stay

8 nights in a Deluxe Room at Sole Mio Hotel & Wellness, with breakfast included and access to fitness, sauna, steam room and wellness facilities.

Explore

Phuket Old Town, beaches, local markets, cultural visits, hosted dinners and a private island-hopping boat day.

Recover

Afternoons can flex around treatments, the pool, beaches and wellness. Optional treatments and activities are available at additional cost.
